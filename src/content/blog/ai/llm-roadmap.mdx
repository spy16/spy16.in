In this series we build a GPT from nothing, in Rust, with zero dependencies. No PyTorch, no NumPy, no `ndarray`. We write the random number generator, the tensors, the autograd engine and the transformer ourselves.

Why Rust instead of Python? In Python, one line of PyTorch hides most of what this series is about. Here nothing is hidden. If a gradient flows somewhere, it's because we wrote the line that sends it there.

Every part is interactive. You can drag the knobs, step through backprop one box at a time, and check every gradient by hand.

## What an LLM actually does

An LLM does exactly one thing: **given the text so far, give a probability for every possible next token.**

<NextTokenLoop />

- **Writing** is that, in a loop: pick a token, append it, ask again.
- **Training** is that, plus a nudge. Say the real next token was " mat", and the model gave it 0.41. The loss is `−log(0.41) = 0.89`. Backprop works out which way each of the 124 million knobs should turn to push 0.41 up, and every knob takes a small step.

Everything in this series serves one of those two loops.

## How each part works

Each part follows the same four steps:

2. **An interactive** to play with until the idea feels obvious.
